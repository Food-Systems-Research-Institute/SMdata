Bibliography
Uniform Crime Reporting Program Data: Arrests by Age, Sex, and Race,
Summarized Yearly, United States, 2012

This bibliography includes published and unpublished works that are related 
to ICPSR study 35018. It includes works that are based on primary
or secondary analysis of the data, or which describe or critique those
data or the collection methodology. 

If you publish a work or know of other works that are based on these data,
please send the complete citation and name of the ICPSR study used to:
icpsr-biblio-notify@umich.edu

7. Martinez, Ruben; Kayitsinga, Jean; Horner, Pilar; Ortiz, Daniel V.,
"Latinos 2025: A Needs Assessment of Latino Communities in Southeast
Michigan." East Lansing, MI: Michigan State University, Julian Samora
Research Institute, Jun 2015.
Abstract: http://www.jsri.msu.edu/recent-reports
Full text PDF: http://www.jsri.msu.edu/upload/Latinos%202025.pdf
This report assesses the well-being of Latinos in Michigan and Southeast
Michigan by identifying community issues that influence their daily lives.
It looks at how Latinos compare to other demographic groups in Southeast
Michigan and across the state of Michigan on six important areas:
education, economic well-being, health and health behaviors, civic
engagement, community well- being and immigration. The analyses in this
report address the following research questions: 1) How does the well-being
of the Latino population compare to other demographic groups? and 2) What
are the critical needs of the Latino communities in Southeast Michigan?
